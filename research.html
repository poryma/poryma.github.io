Research

My research examines social determinants of health and mortality, with a focus on suicide, mental health, community violence, time use, and environmental justice. I use quantitative and computational methods to study how social environments shape individual and population health outcomes, primarily in South Korea and Latin America. Increasingly, I bring a social welfare and social policy lens to this work, asking how services and prevention programs can reach the people most at risk.
